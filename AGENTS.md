# Maintaining this profile

- Keep the silicon identity quiet, readable and compact. Projects lead; tools follow.
- Verify every metric and project status against committed evidence before editing.
- Preserve failed experiments and distinguish logical, routed and sign-off evidence.
- A submission or manuscript is not a publication; generated GDS is not tapeout.
- Forks and open upstream PRs are not merged contributions.
- Keep selected-project copy short and link directly to results or reproduction.
- Do not add badge clouds, counters, streak widgets, animations or external font dependencies.
- Keep assets accessible and readable in light/dark mode and on mobile; retain alt text.
- Use only already-public professional information. Never expose private account data or credentials.
- Retain the license, notice and exact source attribution of the MOTIF trace.
- Keep audit notes, previews, caches and temporary tooling out of this repository.
